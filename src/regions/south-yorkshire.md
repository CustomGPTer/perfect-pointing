South Yorkshire is the far side of the Pennines from us — Penistone and Barnsley are about an hour, Sheffield and Rotherham a little more — and the work there is whole-elevation and whole-house jobs, booked as a block. Penistone and the villages along the Don are gritstone in lime; Barnsley, Wombwell, Hoyland and the pit villages are a mix of older stone and Victorian brick on coalfield ground, where we check for settlement before we quote.

Sheffield is a big stone-and-brick city — Crookes, Walkley, Nether Edge and Broomhill are Victorian stone and brick with conservation areas; Hillsborough, Heeley and Attercliffe are brick terraces — and Rotherham and Doncaster are largely Victorian and inter-war brick. No travel charge within 15 miles of Rochdale; beyond that, it's priced into the fixed quote and shown as one line.
