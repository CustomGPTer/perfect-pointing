Over the top of the Pennines is the other half of our stone work. The upper Calder valley — Todmorden, Hebden Bridge, Sowerby Bridge — is fifteen to thirty minutes from Rochdale and as much home ground as Rossendale is. Halifax, Huddersfield, Brighouse and the Holme and Colne valleys are gritstone in lime with a great deal of cement on it that needs to come off; Bradford, Keighley, Bingley and Shipley are the same story in Airedale, with Saltaire and Haworth as the showpieces; Leeds and Wakefield are where the stone starts to give way to brick.

Beyond thirty miles we cover Castleford, Pontefract, Normanton and Garforth on the coalfield side of Wakefield, Otley, Guiseley and Wetherby north of Leeds, and Harrogate, Knaresborough, Settle and Grassington in North Yorkshire — spa-town stone and Dales limestone, nearly all conservation area, all lime. No travel charge within 15 miles of Rochdale; past that it is priced into the fixed quote on the bigger jobs these tend to be.
