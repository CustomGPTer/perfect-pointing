Lancashire starts a mile north of our yard. Rossendale — Bacup, Rawtenstall, Haslingden — is stone-terrace country in lime, and it is where we do a lot of our week-in, week-out work. Blackburn, Accrington and Hyndburn are the home of the Accrington brick, hard and red and needing a compatible mix; Burnley and Pendle mix stone terraces with brick; the Ribble Valley is limestone and gritstone villages that are almost all conservation area; Chorley, Leyland and Preston are Victorian brick with stone in the villages.

Further out we cover the Fylde — Blackpool, Lytham St Annes, Fleetwood, Poulton, Kirkham — which is brick, a lot of it inter-war, on the wettest, windiest coast in the county, and Lancaster and Morecambe, which are Georgian and Victorian in local sandstone and gritstone. There is no travel charge within 15 miles of Rochdale; beyond that, on the bigger jobs the Fylde and Lancaster tend to be, the travel is priced into the fixed quote and you'll see it as one line.
