Merseyside is brick — Victorian terraces built for the docks, the glass, the coal and the railways, Edwardian villas in the better suburbs, inter-war and post-war estates around them — with red Triassic sandstone on the older churches, walls and villas in Liverpool, Woolton and the Wirral. St Helens, Newton-le-Willows and Prescot on the near side are under an hour from Rochdale and have their own page.

Beyond that we cover Huyton and Kirkby, Liverpool itself — Aigburth, Allerton, Wavertree, Anfield, Walton, Woolton and the conservation areas around Sefton Park and Princes Park — Bootle, Crosby, Formby and Southport up the coast, where the salt wind is hard on pointing, and Birkenhead, Wallasey and Heswall on the Wirral. Whole-elevation and whole-house jobs, booked as a block; no travel charge within 15 miles of Rochdale, and beyond that it's one line on the fixed quote.
