Wilmslow, Alderley Edge and Handforth are Cheshire brick — Edwardian and inter-war detached houses and villas, a lot of them in fine rubbed red brick with stone or terracotta dressings and large chimneys — and the owners want a matched joint, not a smeared one. We mix to a cleaned sample and tool the joint to match the original.

Alderley Edge village and Wilmslow's older streets have conservation areas. About fifty-five minutes from Rochdale via the M60 and A34; the villa jobs here are a week and we book them as a block.

The typical job is a full elevation on an Edwardian house around Fulshaw Park, Hough or the Carrs where the joint colour has to match the original, or a tall chimney stack that has been left too long. Styal village is National Trust and conservation area, and the old brick cottages there are lime work with a very fine joint. Handforth's inter-war semis are bays, gables and chimneys in cement.
