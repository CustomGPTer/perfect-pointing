Saddleworth is lime work, almost without exception. The weavers' cottages and terraces in Uppermill, Delph, Dobcross, Diggle and Greenfield are Millstone Grit, laid in lime, and they have been slowly damaged by generations of cement repointing that traps water in the stone. Faces blow off, the inside wall goes damp and the pointing itself stays stubbornly intact.

Our job here is usually to rake that cement out by hand, repoint in an NHL 3.5 lime and brush it back so the aggregate shows and the joint sits slightly behind the stone. Much of Saddleworth is a conservation area; the villages are a short run up the A669 and A670 from Rochdale and we are up there most months.
