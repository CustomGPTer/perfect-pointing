Accrington is the home of the brick, and the terraces around Church, Clayton-le-Moors and the town centre are built in the hard, red Nori that's pointed nearly everything in Lancashire. It is dense and it does not absorb, so the pointing takes all the weathering — and it needs a mix that is compatible with it, not just strong. We match it.

Oswaldtwistle, Baxenden and the hillside streets towards Haslingden are stone in lime, and Great Harwood and Rishton have both. Church has a conservation area near the canal. About thirty-five minutes from Rochdale via the M66 and M65.
