Hazel Grove is inter-war semis and detached houses on the whole, with older Victorian terraces strung along the A6 and around Norbury and Torkington. Brick, cement, and the same faults as the rest of south Stockport: spalled bricks below the damp course, failed pointing at the bay, and chimneys that have been left too long.

The A6 terraces get traffic grime that hides how bad the pointing is; we clean a section first so we and you can see what's there. A full front on a semi here is a two-to-three-day job. About forty-five minutes from Rochdale — we'll usually book Hazel Grove alongside other Stockport work.
