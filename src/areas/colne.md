Colne is stone terraces climbing the hill, with older weavers' cottages in Trawden, Laneshaw Bridge and Barnoldswick beyond it. All gritstone, all built in lime, most of it cement-pointed at some point and needing that taken out. It is the far end of the M65 from us — about fifty minutes — but a lime job here is several days and we are in Pendle regularly.

The Skipton Road side is exposed and chimneys and north gables go first. The old town around the Market Place and Albert Road is conservation area and the joint profile is expected to match the original.
