Droylsden's older streets around Market Street, Manchester Road and Fairfield are Victorian brick terraces, many with the original tuck-pointed fronts long since cement-slurried over. The Fairfield Moravian settlement is a conservation area and worth treating with care; the rest of the town is standard cement work done to a good standard.

The typical job is a front elevation that's been patched over the years in three different mixes and now needs doing properly, or a gable that has spalled where the neighbour's extension changed how the rain runs. On the inter-war semis towards Audenshaw and Clayton it's bays and chimneys. About half an hour from Rochdale down the A62.
