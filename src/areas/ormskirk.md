Ormskirk is West Lancashire brick — Georgian and Victorian in the town centre around Church Street and Burscough Street, inter-war semis outside it, and old brick cottages in Aughton and Burscough. There's very little stone. The older brick is soft and hand-made and it needs a lime or a weak mix, not a hard modern cement.

The town centre has a conservation area. About an hour from Rochdale on the M62/M58, so Ormskirk is bigger-job territory — a full elevation or a whole house — and we pair it with work in Skem or Wigan.
