Widnes is Victorian brick terraces around the town centre, Farnworth village, West Bank and the older streets, with inter-war and post-war semis around them. Cement work throughout; the Mersey side is damp and windy and the west and south elevations carry the damage.

About an hour from Rochdale via the M62. Whole-elevation jobs, grouped with Warrington, Runcorn and St Helens.

The typical Widnes job is a full front on a terrace where the pointing has been patched in three different colours over the years and the owner wants it to look like one wall again. We clean a section first to get the colour match, rake the lot out to a clean depth, replace any frost-spalled bricks and repoint in one consistent joint. Chimneys on the older streets are usually added while the tower is up, because they are rarely better than the wall below.
