Skelmersdale is mostly 1960s and 1970s new-town housing — brick, often with recessed joints that were never full — with the original Victorian terraces of Old Skem around High Street and the stone village of Upholland next door. The new-town brick is now sixty years old and the pointing on it is failing in the same way the estates in Manchester are.

We repoint the new-town houses to the original recessed profile, do cement work on the Victorian terraces and lime on the Upholland stone. About fifty-five minutes from Rochdale via the M62 and M58, so we group Skem with Wigan and St Helens.
