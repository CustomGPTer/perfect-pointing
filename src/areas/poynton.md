Poynton was a colliery village before it was a commuter town, and the older terraces around Park Lane and London Road are Victorian brick built for the pits, with inter-war and post-war semis and detached houses around them. Cement work, and a check for old-workings settlement on the Victorian streets before quoting.

Adlington and Woodford are Cheshire brick cottages, some of them old enough to be in lime. About fifty minutes from Rochdale; grouped with Hazel Grove and Macclesfield.

The commuter-era detached houses around Poynton Park and towards Higher Poynton are a mix of buff and red facing brick from the 1960s and 70s with recessed joints that have weathered thin; we repoint to the original profile so it doesn't show. On the older village streets the job is a full front, replacing spalled bricks and re-bedding cills, with the chimney done from the same tower. Poynton has a conservation area around the village centre.
