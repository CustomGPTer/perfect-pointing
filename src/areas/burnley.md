Burnley is a mix. The terraces around Rosegrove, Burnley Wood, Harle Syke and the Weavers' Triangle are mostly stone in lime; the later streets and the Padiham side have a lot of Accrington brick, which is hard, dense and wants a matched mix rather than a generic one. We look before we quote. Thirty-five minutes from Rochdale over Todmorden or via the M65.

Rosehill and Manchester Road have larger Victorian villas with carved stone that need careful lime work. Padiham and Hapton are stone cottages. Burnley's weather is Pennine weather and the west and north gables suffer; chimneys on the terraces are frequently worse than the walls. Parts of the town are conservation areas.
