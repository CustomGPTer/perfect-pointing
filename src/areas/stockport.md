Stockport is big enough to have every wall type we work with. The old town around the Market Place and Underbank has stone and very old brick in lime; Heaton Moor, Heaton Mersey and Davenport have large Edwardian villas with decorative red brick and stone dressings; Edgeley, Shaw Heath and Cale Green are grids of Victorian terraces; and the outer estates are inter-war and post-war semis.

We quote each on its own merits. The Heatons in particular have owners who want the joint matched exactly to the original — colour, width and profile — and that is what we do, mixing on site. The Heatons and the town centre have conservation areas. Forty minutes from Rochdale via the M60.
