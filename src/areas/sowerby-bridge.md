Sowerby Bridge, Ripponden and Luddenden Foot are stone towns on the Calder — gritstone terraces along the river and canal, mill housing up the hillsides, older cottages in the villages above — and the work is lime. The canal side is damp and the lower courses show it; up the hill it is the wind and the north gables.

We rake out the cement, point in lime and brush it back to match the older sections. Sowerby Bridge town centre and Ripponden village are conservation areas. About thirty minutes from Rochdale over the tops on the A58.
