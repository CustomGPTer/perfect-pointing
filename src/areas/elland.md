Elland, Greetland, Stainland and West Vale are stone — the terraces around Elland town centre, the mill housing along the Calder and the older cottages on Elland Edge — with a bit of brick on the later streets. Lime for the stone, cement for the brick, and a lot of cement to take off stone that never should have had it.

The hillside streets and Elland Edge are exposed to the west and the gables carry the damage. Thirty-five minutes from Rochdale via the M62, and we're often in Halifax and Huddersfield on the same day.
