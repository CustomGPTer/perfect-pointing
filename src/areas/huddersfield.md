Huddersfield's housing is stone — honey-coloured gritstone that goes almost black on exposed faces — and it was all built in lime. The terraces of Lockwood, Crosland Moor, Paddock and Moldgreen, the villas of Edgerton, Lindley and Birkby, the mill housing of Milnsbridge, Slaithwaite and Marsden in the Colne Valley: all lime work, and most of it has cement on that needs to come off.

Edgerton, Lindley and the Colne Valley villages have conservation areas. We rake by hand, point in NHL 3.5 lime with a matched aggregate and finish flush or slightly recessed and brushed. Forty minutes from Rochdale on the M62, and a job here is usually several days.
