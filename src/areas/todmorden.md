Todmorden is fifteen minutes up the A6033 and it is as close to home as Littleborough. Everything is stone — terraces stacked up the valley sides at Walsden, Cornholme, Lydgate and Shade, weavers' cottages up on the tops — and everything was built in lime. Most of it has been cement-pointed since and the town is full of stone that is blowing because of it.

The work is lime, raked by hand and brushed back. Todmorden's town centre is a conservation area and the pointing profile is expected to match. Access is the thing here: stepped streets, tight backs, houses half-buried in the hill. We'll say at the quote how we'll reach the wall.
