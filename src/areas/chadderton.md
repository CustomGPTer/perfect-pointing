Chadderton is ten minutes down the A663 and about as close to home as it gets. The housing is nearly all brick — Edwardian terraces off Middleton Road and around Chadderton Hall, inter-war semis on the Foxdenton side and post-war estates at Firwood Park and Whitegate — so most of what we do here is cement repointing in a 4:1 mix, weather struck or bucket handle to match the street.

The usual call-out is frost-spalled bricks below the damp course on a north-facing gable, or hollow joints around a bay window that have started letting water into the front room. Both are half-day-to-two-day jobs. The Broadway side gets the weather off Oldham Edge, so chimneys there go earlier than you'd expect.
