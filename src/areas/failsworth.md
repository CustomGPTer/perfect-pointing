Failsworth sits on the Rochdale Canal between Oldham and Manchester, and the older streets either side of Oldham Road are classic Lancashire red-brick terraces with cills and lintels in stone. Cement pointing suits them; what doesn't suit them is the hard, grey, over-filled repointing a lot of them were given in the 1970s, which is now cracking away and taking the brick face with it.

We rake that out properly rather than skimming over it. On the semis around Woodhouses and Holt Lane the work is more often chimneys and gables, and on the newer canal-side housing it's snagging — recessed joints that were never full in the first place. Twenty minutes from Rochdale, so no travel added to the price.
