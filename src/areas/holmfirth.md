Holmfirth, Honley, New Mill and Netherthong are Holme Valley stone — weavers' cottages, terraces stepped up the hill, big square millowners' houses — all gritstone, all originally lime. The valley is a conservation area in large part and the expectation is a lime repoint with a matched aggregate and the original joint profile, which is what we do.

Cement removal by hand is slow and it's the bulk of the job. The valley is exposed to the south-west and the gables and chimneys carry the weather. About fifty minutes from Rochdale via the M62 and Huddersfield; jobs here run to a week and the drive is not an issue.
