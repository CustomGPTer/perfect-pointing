Clitheroe and the Ribble Valley villages — Whalley, Waddington, Downham, Chatburn, Sabden — are stone in lime, and a lot of it is limestone rather than the gritstone we see nearer home. Limestone is unforgiving with cement and the villages are conservation areas almost without exception, so the work is careful lime repointing with a matched aggregate and a flush or slightly recessed finish.

Clitheroe's Victorian terraces around the castle and the station are gritstone. The valley is wetter than most; north and west elevations carry the damage. About fifty minutes from Rochdale; jobs here are typically three days or more.
