Rochdale is where Perfect Pointing started in 2000 and where most of our work still is. The town has every kind of wall we point: gritstone terraces around Spotland, Wardle and Norden that need lime; Victorian and Edwardian brick around Castleton and Milkstone; 1930s semis in Bamford and Castleton; and post-war estates in Kirkholt and Belfield where cement repointing is right. We know which is which before we've got out of the van, and the quote reflects it.

Being local means Scott can usually survey within a few days, and we're not adding travel to the price. Chimneys catch the weather badly on the Pennine side of town — if you've got a damp patch on an upstairs wall facing the hills, that's usually where to look.

The regular jobs: full elevations on Victorian terraces around Milkstone, Deeplish and Newbold; frost-spalled bricks cut out and replaced on the 1930s semis of Bamford, Marland and Castleton; gritstone garden and boundary walls in Norden, Bamford and Healey re-bedded and repointed in lime; and chimney stacks everywhere from Spotland to Smallbridge. Wardle, Healey and the Syke side of town are in the weather and the stone terraces up there are almost always lime-pointing work.

If you're in the OL11, OL12 or OL16 postcodes you're on our doorstep — Scott can usually call round within a few days, and small jobs get fitted in between the larger contracts rather than waiting for a slot.
