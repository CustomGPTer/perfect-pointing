Macclesfield is a silk town and the terraces around the centre, Hurdsfield and the canal are Georgian and Victorian brick, some of the older ones in lime; the hillside villages of Bollington, Rainow and Kerridge are Pennine gritstone. Two different jobs and we carry both mixes.

The town centre and Bollington have conservation areas. About an hour from Rochdale via the M60 and A523; multi-day jobs, grouped with Poynton and Wilmslow.
