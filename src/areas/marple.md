Marple and Marple Bridge sit where the brick of Stockport meets the stone of the Peak, and the canal-side cottages, the old streets around Marple Bridge and Compstall and the houses up towards Mellor are gritstone in lime. Cement has been put on most of them at some point and it does the same damage here as it does in Saddleworth — trapped water, blown faces, damp inside.

We rake it out and lime-point them. The Victorian villas and inter-war semis nearer the centre are brick and take cement. Compstall and Marple Bridge are conservation areas. About forty-five minutes from Rochdale; a lime job here is usually three to six days so the drive is not an issue.
