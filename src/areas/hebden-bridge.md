Hebden Bridge's over-and-under terraces, the streets up towards Heptonstall and the cottages in Mytholmroyd and Cragg Vale are all gritstone in lime, and the whole town centre is a conservation area. Cement repointing has done the same damage here as everywhere else in the upper Calder valley and we spend a lot of time taking it back out.

The job is slow hand raking and lime repointing with a matched aggregate, flush or slightly recessed, brushed. Access on the stacked streets is often from the road below with a tower, or from the neighbour's yard by arrangement. About twenty-five minutes from Rochdale over Blackstone Edge.
