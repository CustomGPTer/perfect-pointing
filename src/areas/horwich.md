Horwich is half stone and half brick. Up towards Rivington and the moor the older cottages and terraces are gritstone in lime; down in the town the long grid of terraces built for the Loco Works is red brick. Blackrod next door is mostly stone. We carry both mixes and the wall decides which.

The Rivington side is properly exposed and the stone houses there have suffered from cement repointing more than most; we rake it out by hand and use lime. On the Loco Works terraces it is cement, usually a full front or a street run, with the chimneys done while the tower is up. Forty minutes from Rochdale via the M61.
