Denton grew on hatting and the terraces around the town centre, Haughton Green and Dane Bank are late-Victorian brick built for hat workers, solid and plain. They repoint well in a bucket-handle joint. The bigger Edwardian houses off Manchester Road have rubbed-brick arches and more decorative work, which needs a finer joint and a bit more care.

The most common job is a gable end that has never been done and is now shedding sand, usually combined with the chimney that sits on it. On the inter-war estates towards Audenshaw it's front elevations and bays. Half an hour from Rochdale, straight down the M60.
