Runcorn is two towns: the Victorian brick terraces of the Old Town around Halton Road and the canal, and the 1960s and 1970s new-town estates behind them. Halton village on the hill has older stone in lime. The new-town brick has the same failing recessed joints as Skelmersdale; the Old Town is standard cement work.

About an hour from Rochdale via the M62. Whole-elevation jobs only, paired with Widnes and Warrington.

The 1960s and 70s estates — Halton Lodge, Castlefields, Murdishaw, Palacefields — have brick with recessed joints that were sprayed or raked thin when built and have now weathered back to nothing in places. We repoint them to the original recessed profile so the repaired areas match, rather than filling them flush and leaving an obvious patch. On the Old Town terraces the usual job is a front elevation, chimneys and re-bedded cills.
