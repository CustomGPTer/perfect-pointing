Dewsbury, Thornhill, Earlsheaton, Ravensthorpe and Batley Carr are gritstone in lime — the mill terraces along the Calder, the hillside streets above the town, the bigger Victorian houses towards Thornhill — and most of it has been cement-pointed since. We rake that out by hand and lime-point.

Dewsbury town centre has a conservation area. Fifty minutes from Rochdale on the M62; we pair Dewsbury with Mirfield, Batley and Huddersfield and take on full elevations and whole houses.

Dewsbury's terraces are often stepped down steep streets — Thornhill Lees, Earlsheaton, up towards Hanging Heaton — and the gable at the bottom of the run takes the weather for the whole row. Those gables and the chimneys on them are the usual first job. The bigger Victorian houses around Ravensthorpe and Savile Town have carved stone dressings and deep joints that want a slightly recessed lime finish, not a flush cement one.
