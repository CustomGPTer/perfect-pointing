Rawtenstall, Cloughfold, Crawshawbooth and Waterfoot are Rossendale stone — big, square, gritstone terraces and villas that were built in lime and have mostly been cement-pointed since. The work is lime repointing, hand-raked, and we do a lot of it in the valley. Twenty minutes from Rochdale up the A681.

The bigger Victorian villas around Cloughfold and Newchurch have carved stone dressings and deep joints that want a slightly recessed lime finish; the terraces on the hillsides above the town get the weather and lose their gable pointing first. Newchurch and parts of the town centre are conservation areas.
