Bradford is stone — the gritstone terraces of Manningham, Girlington, Great Horton and Bowling, the big Victorian villas of Heaton and Frizinghall, the warehouses of Little Germany — and it was built in lime. Cement has been put on nearly all of it and Bradford has more blown stone faces per street than anywhere we work. The cure is slow: rake it out by hand and put lime back.

Saltaire is a World Heritage Site and Little Germany, Heaton and Manningham have conservation areas; the pointing has to match the original profile. Fifty minutes from Rochdale on the M62 and M606; we take on whole-elevation and whole-house lime work in the city.
