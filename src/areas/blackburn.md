Blackburn is Accrington brick more than anywhere — dense red Nori terraces around Audley, Bank Top, Mill Hill and Ewood — with stone in the older streets near the cathedral, along the Leeds and Liverpool Canal and up towards Pleasington and Whalley. Nori is hard and unforgiving; the mix has to be compatible and the joint has to be tooled properly or it fails at the edge.

Preston New Road, Beardwood and Revidge have larger Victorian villas with carved stone dressings that want lime and a fine joint. Full front elevations and street runs are common here. About forty minutes from Rochdale via the M66 and M65, and we take on whole-house jobs.
