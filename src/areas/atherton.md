Atherton's terraces around Hindsford, Howe Bridge and the town centre were built for colliery and mill workers in the late 1800s and they are solid, plain red brick. Cement repointing with a bucket-handle joint suits them and it's most of what we do here. Howe Bridge is a conservation area — the model village — and wants the original joint kept.

Colliery settlement shows in cracked lintels and stepped cracks on some streets; we'll tell you whether the wall needs a structural repair before pointing or just the pointing. The inter-war semis along the A577 and around Leigh Road are bays, gables and chimneys. Thirty-five minutes from Rochdale.
