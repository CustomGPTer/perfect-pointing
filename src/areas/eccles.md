Eccles and Monton have a lot of well-built Edwardian brick — the villas and big semis around Monton Green and Half Edge Lane, and the terraces off Liverpool Road and Church Street — with the decorative brickwork and stone cills that need a proper, fine joint rather than a trowel-full of cement pushed in. We match the joint width and profile of the original.

Winton and Peel Green are inter-war semis and the work there is full fronts, bays and chimneys. Barton and the Ship Canal side gets damp; the bottom courses of the older houses often need a few bricks cutting out before the pointing is worth doing. Half an hour from Rochdale on the M60.
