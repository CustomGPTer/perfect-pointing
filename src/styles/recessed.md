A recessed joint is raked back a few millimetres from the face of the brick and left square or slightly rounded. The shadow it casts makes each brick stand out and gives the wall a sharp, modern look.

The trade-off is weather resistance. The top edge of each brick is left exposed and the ledge holds water, so on soft brick or exposed walls a recessed joint speeds up frost damage. Developers use it on new build because modern engineering brick can take it.

We'll match a recessed profile on newer houses — snagging repoints on developer homes in Milnrow and Heywood, for instance — and we'll advise against it on anything older.
