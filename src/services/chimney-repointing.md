Stacks take the worst of the weather and are usually the source of a damp patch on a bedroom wall. We repoint the stack, renew cracked flaunching around the pots, re-bed loose pots and check the lead flashings and back gutter while we're up there. Worked from a scaffold tower or roof ladders with proper edge protection.
