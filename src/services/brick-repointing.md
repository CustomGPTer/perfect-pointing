Joints raked out to a minimum of 15–20 mm, dust cleaned out, wall dampened, then repointed in a 4:1 or 5:1 sand and cement mix matched to the colour and texture of your existing joints. Struck to the profile already on the house — weather struck, bucket handle, flush or recessed.
